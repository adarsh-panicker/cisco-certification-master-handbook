### How to Report an Errata

If you find a typo, command syntax discrepancy, or ambiguous explanation:
1. Navigate to [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/adarsh-panicker/cisco-certification-master-handbook/issues).
2. Click **New Issue**.
3. Select the **Errata Submission** template.
4. Fill in the chapter, section, snippet, and suggested correction.
