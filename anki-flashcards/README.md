# Interactive Anki Spaced-Repetition Flashcard Deck
### Cisco Certification Master Study Handbook — Digital Study Companion

This directory contains the official pre-formatted, digital spaced-repetition flashcard deck for **Anki** (desktop, mobile, and web).

---

## 📦 Deck Contents (400+ Cards)

The deck covers:
1. **Master Glossary Terms**: Over 180 enterprise networking terms with architectural context ([A] CCNA, [E] CCNP ENCOR, [R] CCNP ENARSI).
2. **Administrative Distance Rankings**: All 12 route sources from Connected (0) to Untrusted (255).
3. **Protocol Timers & Keepalives**: OSPF, EIGRP, BGP, HSRP, VRRP, GLBP, STP, and RSTP defaults.
4. **Well-Known Exam Ports & Protocols**: Over 20 protocol port numbers, transport layers, and protocol IDs.
5. **Chapter Practice Questions**: All 70 scenario-based questions from Chapters 1 through 14 with answer keys and technical rationales.
6. **Full Mock Exam Questions**: All 60 exam questions across CCNA 200-301, CCNP ENCOR 350-401, and CCNP ENARSI 300-410.
7. **Production Pro-Tips & Field Tricks**: High-stakes engineering pitfalls and exam traps.

## 📲 How to Import into Anki

### Method 1: Using Anki Desktop (Recommended)
1. Download and open [Anki Desktop](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) (Windows / macOS / Linux).
2. Click **File** -> **Import...** (or press `Ctrl+I`).
3. Select `cisco_master_study_flashcards_anki.csv` or `cisco_master_study_flashcards_anki.txt`.
4. In the Import Dialog:
   - **Type**: Basic
   - **Deck**: Create or select `Cisco Master Study Handbook`
   - **Field mapping**:
     - Field 1 $\rightarrow$ Front
     - Field 2 $\rightarrow$ Back
     - Field 3 $\rightarrow$ Tags
   - Check the box: **Allow HTML in fields** (to preserve formatting and bold highlights).
5. Click **Import**.

### Method 2: Using AnkiWeb or AnkiMobile
You can import the deck into Anki Desktop and synchronize with your free [AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net) account to review flashcards on your iPhone, iPad, or Android phone.
